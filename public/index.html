The sound

My sound goes from a calm 124 BPM all the way up to techno, in many different styles. That's why The Drift3r: one mood can change the style.

128 BPM is my personal favourite, bringing people together in a tight, neat way. That's my goal. Unity could be my passion, not proven yet.
